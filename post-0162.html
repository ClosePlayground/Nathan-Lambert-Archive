Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Thank you to The Wilderness Society and Darebin Climate Action Now for organising tonight’s candidates’ forum on climate and nature. The topics discussed included: — decarbonisation — data centres — new gas projects in Bass Strait — emissions from livestock and fertilisers — funding for Traditional Owner ranger programs, and — the regulation of planned burns, salvage logging and private forests. Tomorrow, Hands on Health Australia will be hosting a crime and community safety forum on Edwardes Street. Check out their Instagram account for details.
