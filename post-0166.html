Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

We got there! The Gellibrand Crescent Kindergarten expansion has been locked in, with Darebin Council agreeing to jointly fund the construction of a second kinder room with the Victorian Labor Government. The expansion will provide 40 additional places, along with upgrades to the existing kinder room. A huge thank you to Minister for Children Lizzie Blandthorn, Darebin Council, and the Northern Darebin Combined Kindergarten Association for helping make this happen! Construction won’t start until the next term of government (with the new room expected to open in 2029) but the critical funding commitments have been signed off. We have also followed up regarding the closure of day care at Merrilands Uniting. There is unlikely to be any change to Uniting’s decision, but if anyone would like further information, please get in touch. — with Minister Lizzie Blandthorn, Mayor Emily Dimitriadis, Tracey Attard from the Northern Darebin Combined Kindergarten Association, and some fantastic young kinder kids!
