The NCAT hangar is something to behold! Thanks to NCAT principal Raffaela Galati-Brown and school council president Danielle Parker for showing us some of the students’ latest construction projects — as well as other impressive work in visual arts, photography, integrated technologies and musical instrument making. Raffaela and Danielle also raised some important issues on behalf of the school community, including: — the exposure of the hangar to the elements — the state of portable classrooms — the need for a dedicated student centre, and — recognition of NCAT’s specialist role, including through its zoning and designation. If you know any young people interested in the arts, technologies or trades, it’s worth coming down to NCAT on Murray Road near Northland and having a look. — with Raffaela Galati-Brown and Danielle Parker at the Northern College of the Arts & Technology
