PRESTON MARKET UPDATE — submissions to Engage Victoria close at 5 pm today.

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

PRESTON MARKET UPDATE — submissions to Engage Victoria close at 5 pm today. We have received further answers to some of our questions about financial support for traders, East Block heritage, gas connections, etc. The general answer is that these issues will be addressed at the planning permit stage. But if you would like more details, please get in touch and we can email the full answers through. The government will enter ‘caretaker mode’ on 3 November, but we are hoping to get a high-level summary of the submissions before then. As previously discussed, detailed assessment and decision-making around the development plan will be a matter for the next term of government.
