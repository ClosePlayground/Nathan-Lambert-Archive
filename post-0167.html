Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Doorknocking report for Roseberry Ave, Townhall Ave, and the homes between Gower, Murray, High and Plenty… — A key issue in Roseberry was the decision by council to discontinue the free neighbourhood street party program. This has affected the Roseberry Ave Christmas party as well as other street parties, with potential fees now of $500-$1,000. We have asked council to reconsider. — Another issue was the lack of open green space. Unfortunately, council’s ‘Breathing Space’ report treats the astro turf in front of the library as green space (!), which hides the fact that there is a big gap for people looking for a park or playground without having to travel to GH Mott, Wood St or the LXRP playground. One suggestion was to use the old RSL land on Kelvin Grove as a park, or the old police station grounds, or both! — The plans for Preston Market were a common topic. Key concerns included the scale of development, the need to retain the market’s character, and whether the proposed landscaping and design elements would be delivered. — We also discussed the controversial protected bike path proposal for Gower St. We need better east-west cycling connections, and there is nowhere else in Preston for an east-west connection to go, but on the other hand, there is also nowhere for the cars to go on that stretch. One possible solution is a 30 kph zone instead of the protected path. — The complexity of parking arrangements at Sacred Heart Primary came up again. Everyone welcomes the new crossing, but the five-minute parking limit is very short! — Most people thought the situation with LMCT had improved, albeit with continued frustration about promotional pricing days and vehicles turning right into the station from Gower. — Other issues included the need for hockey stick marks on Roseberry, whether residential parking permits were being sold to High St traders, the condition of High St shops, the need to plant more trees, the need to preserve local heritage, the need to build more local public housing, rental costs, gas taxation, Airbnbs, and the appropriate speed limit for High St (upon which there are mixed views!).
