PRESTON MARKET UPDATE — we expect the proposed development plan to be released later this week. We followed up last week’s questions at a briefing today. It turns out that the draft plans align with the ‘Heart of Preston’ proposal submitted to the Standing Advisory Committee by council officers, NOT the version approved by councillors in 2021. That means that the draft plan: — seeks to migrate the entire site to all-electric over time, with no new gas connections, but does not immediately ban all gas; — retains all of the market north of Earle Street, but not the south shed; — allows buildings up to 16 storeys, not 12, albeit with a smaller number of total apartments (as per the video); — provides 10% affordable housing, not 20%, with the delivery model to be determined closer to the planning permit stage; — provides 8% of the site as ground-level public open space, with additional elevated green space; and — includes a potential new kindergarten or childcare centre, but not an MCH centre. The development plan will be made available to the public through the Engage Victoria website so that everyone has the opportunity to review it and provide feedback directly to the Minister for Planning. For those interested, the key design teams on the project are Six Degrees Architects, OCULUS, Macroplan, Mosquito Architecture and Sean Godsell Architects.
