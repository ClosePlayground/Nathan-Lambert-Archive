Getting health advice in a safe, confidential and non-judgemental environment isn’t always easy for…

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Getting health advice in a safe, confidential and non-judgemental environment isn’t always easy for young people. The Doctors in Secondary Schools program addresses that by giving students free access at school to GP services, pathology tests, and referrals to health and mental health specialists. It is an initiative of the Victorian Labor Government, with funding support from the Federal Labor Government. Thank you to Mariam, Jacinta, Matt and Principal Katie Watmough for your leadership in delivering this program at Reservoir High School and advocating for it more broadly. — with Ged Kearney
