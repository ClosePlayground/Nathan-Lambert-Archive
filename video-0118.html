Work is underway at Edgars Road and Mahoneys Road to build a dedicated left-turn lane, improving traffic flow

Source media filename: 1702284564518486.mp4

Video description

Work is underway at Edgars Road and Mahoneys Road to build a dedicated left-turn lane, improving traffic flow and safety. The project will also: — realign and install a pedestrian crossing controlled by traffic lights across the left turn slip lane on Edgars Road — install mast arms to make the traffic lights more visible to motorists approaching the intersection — install safety barriers on the centre island and left-hand side of Edgars Road to prevent side-on crashes — install a bike off-ramp on Edgars Road near Horne Street so bike riders use the intersection safely — install new street lights and signs at the intersection and resurface sections of Edgars Road and Mahoneys Road. Construction is expected to finish in late 2026.
