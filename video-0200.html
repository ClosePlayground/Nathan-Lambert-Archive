Talking about the oval! For reasons we don’t fully understand, approximately 20,000 cubic metres of soil was dumped on the William Ruthven ovals between 2013 and 2015 (though there may have already been earlier fill on the site). Reports describe the soil as “uncontrolled fill”, meaning there is no documented engineering control over how it was placed or compacted. The soil is also predominantly clay. It was not ever likely to create a good sporting surface. If anyone has any further information, please let us know. Unfortunately, fixing the problem will require significant and costly earthworks. -- with Premier @[100058101160143:2048:Ben Carroll MP] at @[100063581503427:2048:William Ruthven Secondary College]
