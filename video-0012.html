Reservoir is a very big suburb, and people sometimes ask, “Is it Melbourne’s biggest?”. The short answer is “No, but it used to be”. Reservoir is the largest suburb by area within 25 km of the CBD, and as it filled out, it became the largest by population, probably at some point in the 1970s. However, it was overtaken by Craigieburn in 2016. It now sits 6th on the list.
