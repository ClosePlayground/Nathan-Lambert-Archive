Any development at Preston Market would require two things: approved planning permits and an approved ‘development plan’ — in effect, a blueprint for how the whole site would work. The development plan would include details of: — the location and design of any new buildings; — the protection of the existing market buildings; — the location of new open space, landscaping and trees; — the staging of any works; and — support for traders during any construction. We have asked at what point such a development plan would be made public.
