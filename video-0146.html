Most linear reserves in Preston and Reservoir have water pipes or underground creeks beneath them.

Source media filename: 2211297363049390.mp4

Video description

Most linear reserves in Preston and Reservoir have water pipes or underground creeks beneath them. This is often used as a reason why walking paths or vegetation cannot be added, even though other parts of Melbourne have paths and planting over similar infrastructure. We have asked the Minister for Water about developing a more consistent approach so local reserves can be better used by the community. Relevant local reserves include: — Northern Pipe Trail (@[100064863125422:2048:Melbourne Water] pipes), — Regent Linear Reserve (Melbourne Water and @[100064783356076:2048:Yarra Valley Water] pipes), — Maroondah Aqueduct Linear Reserve (Melbourne Water pipes), — Ruthven Linear Reserve (underground creek), — Elsey Road Linear Reserve (underground creek), — Union Reserve (underground creek) and — Camera Walk near the Coburg Hill shops (Melbourne Water pipes).
