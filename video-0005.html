Today, we tabled the @[100071960739450:2048:Save the Preston Market] petition. Some of the signatures were from last year, and some things have changed since then, but the general message of the petition hasn't. The petition had 4,693 valid signatures (ie, handwritten signatures accompanied by a full name and full address). That makes it the largest petition tabled in parliament in the last three years. (UPDATE: as per the comments below, I should clarify that in addition to the 4,963 valid signatures there were also approximately 1,928 handwritten signatures that didn't include their full details and 4,815 typed signatures for a total of 11,490 signatures across over 1,500 pages. It was a very big petition!) (Also thanks to Jake who was doing work experience for helping count them all, if that's not a classic work experience task I don't know what is 😊)
