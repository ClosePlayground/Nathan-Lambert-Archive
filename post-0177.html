Scooping a creek for water bugs is a good way to check its health. Finding a diversity of aquatic creatures is an encouraging sign — especially when they include species known to be sensitive to pollution. Today we joined the Darebin Creek Management Committee for their annual Waterwatch survey at Darebin Parklands. We found caddisfly larvae and other pollution-sensitive creatures — a moment of excitement for us, and a credit to the committee’s efforts to keep the creek healthy! As mentioned in our previous post, a re-elected Carroll Labor Government will provide the Darebin Creek and Merri Creek management committees with a further $300,000 to help them continue their important work. — with Kat Theophanous MP and the amazing Darebin Creek Management Committee team, including Peter Grenfell, Kim Davis, Danny Reddan and Annette Salkeld
