COST OF LIVING RELIEF — a reminder that applications for the Victorian Labor Government’s 20% rego rebate will open on Monday 1 June through the Service Victoria app and website, and close on Friday 31 July. Along with free public transport and half-price fares for the rest of the year, this rebate is helping Victorians manage the cost-of-living impacts associated with the conflict in the Middle East. The rebate applies to eligible vehicles registered in an individual’s name. It is not available for vehicles registered to companies.
