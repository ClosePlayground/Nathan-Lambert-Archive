Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Three-year-old Daisy and her parents, Rebecca and Alistair, are Reservoir locals who are helping make Victorians healthier. Daisy was born prematurely and spent her first three months in the NICU. While she was there, her family signed up to GenV — a Victorian Government-supported project that brings together data from thousands of children and families to help researchers understand why children have different starts to life. At a time when medical research is being undermined and defunded in the United States, we welcome any opportunity to show support for our fantastic local medical researchers and the vital work they do! Happy fifth birthday, GenV! We hope this project continues with strong government support for many years to come. — with Alistair and Daisy, Rebecca and Matilda, Prof Lisa Hui, Dino Asproloupos from Murdoch Children's Research Institute - MCRI and Naomi Schwarz from Generation Victoria - GenV
