The Northern Panthers have been part of Reservoir since 1977, bringing together kids from diverse backgrounds

Video description

The Northern Panthers have been part of Reservoir since 1977, bringing together kids from diverse backgrounds to enjoy rugby — including future stars Christian Leali’ifano, Rob Leota, Josh Coward, Junior Laloifi, Tegan French and Silei Poluleuligaga. Thanks to club president Grant Wason and secretary Jo Tevaga for all the work they do to support the players and families, and for chatting to us about their plans for improved lighting at CH Sullivan Memorial Reserve to support the continued development of women’s and girls’ rugby. — with Grant Wason and @[100063848375357:2048:Mayor Emily Dimitriadis] at @[100063508001179:2048:Northern Panthers Rugby Union Club] UPDATE: some more historical information from the club, for those interested... The club played its first season in 1977 at Donath Reserve. The ground was on the eastern side of Cheddar Road, now occupied by Keon Park FC. The basketball stadium acted as the club rooms, though the Junction Hotel was the unofficial club room. The Club was formed in 1976, and fielded a team in a sevens tournament, which they won. The following year was the first full fifteen-a-side competitive team entry. So next year will mark fifty years of competition.
