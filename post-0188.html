Doorknocking report for the homes between Cheddar Road, Dole Avenue and Elinda Place…

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Doorknocking report for the homes between Cheddar Road, Dole Avenue and Elinda Place… — A number of families have children at Reservoir Views Primary and were pleased about the new crossings on Aberdeen and Greenock (as per photos). The crossings were funded by the Victorian Labor government but delivered by Darebin Council. Some raised concerns about the changes to the Hickford-Borrie intersection. We understand council is taking another look at it. Hopefully the new pedestrian crossings will be sufficiently raised to slow drivers down! — Other road-related issues included the potholes at the southern end of Cheddar Road (which we have raised with council!), the need for speed humps on Dumbarton, and the general challenges of north-south traffic flow in Reservoir. — We also discussed the old kindergarten at 2 Dole Avenue, which has not been used since 2014. As is often the case, there is a good write-up of this issue on Serena O’Meley’s website (the write-up is from 2021 on Darebin Independent Observer but not much has changed!). — Someone raised the very specific issue of older residents from Latrobe Retirement Village and Summerhill Residential Park accessing the Summerhill shopping centre from the Gremel Road side. Apparently, all of the closest entries only have stairs. I guess there is a lift somewhere in the underground car park? Will have to look! — We also discussed crime, community safety, rubbish dumping, the new social housing project on Johnson Street, vacant homes, Preston Market, the Clifton Hill bottleneck, the responsiveness of Darebin Council to Snap Send Solve, hard rubbish arrangements, and better lighting for the car park near IW Dole (aka the home of Chicken HQ). We also ran into Rick Arturi of Arturi family fame. Check out Rick & Jessie’s HMS for all your OC, deck restoration, gardening and landscaping needs! Streets doorknocked: the area enclosed by Cheddar Road, Dole Avenue and Elinda Place, including Daleglen Street, Elizabeth Court, Locher Avenue and Lucille Avenue, plus a little of Dumbarton and Aberdeen.
