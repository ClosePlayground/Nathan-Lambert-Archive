Merrilands Uniting Early Learning will cease providing long day care from 2027. Below is a summary of what we know about the issue. — Approximately 38 families will be affected. — Uniting says “the centre has been struggling to attract families for the last four to five years” and believes this is linked to “an increase in new childcare centres nearby”. — However, families have reported that it has been difficult to obtain daycare places. — Furthermore, it is not clear why a well-regarded, long-standing local centre would lose daycare enrolments to newer centres with typically higher fees. — The Department of Education and Darebin Council are working to assist families to find alternative long day care. — @[100064262696580:2048:Uniting Vic.Tas] has been running childcare at the centre since 1985. — The centre is covered by VECTEA (the Victorian Early Childhood Teachers and Educators Agreement). We are seeking further information regarding the decision.
