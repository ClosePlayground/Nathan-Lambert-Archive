Road safety upgrades! This week’s Darebin Council budget confirmed a number of new projects, supported by the Victorian Labor Government and Federal Labor Government, including: — a proposed raised pedestrian crossing on Cramer Street near Preston West Primary, — proposed new pedestrian crossings around Greenock Street, Aberdeen Street and Darebin Boulevard near Reservoir Views Primary, — a proposed pedestrian crossing upgrade on McColl Street near Holy Name Primary, — Federal Black Spot safety improvements, including safer bike lanes, along Gilbert Road between Edwardes Street and Union Street (thanks Ged Kearney!), — new signalised intersections replacing the roundabouts at Gilbert/Henty and Gilbert/Edwardes, — Federal Black Spot funding for a safer intersection at Arundel Avenue and Finton Grove, — design funding for a safer crossing on Yarra Avenue outside Reservoir East Primary (thanks Cr Vasilios Tsalkos), — funding to resurface Legs Lane near Reservoir Primary (thanks to RPS parents Daniel and Emeline), and — speed bumps on Radford Road. The Victorian Government is also supporting Darebin Council’s Safe Routes to School program at Sacred Heart Primary.
