Do you have any feedback on the new intersection at Hughes Parade? So far, we’ve heard that: — it’s much better for access to the station, — it’s safer for turning right, — there are unfortunately some motorists who are running the red lights (not sure why this happens with new intersections but it does seem to happen quite often), — there is a bit of confusion about the pedestrian and bike crossings, especially in the north-west corner where they aren’t synced, and — people would like the southern side to be a pedestrian crossing as well. Overall, the feedback has been very positive but if there is anything you think we should pass on to the project team, let us know.
