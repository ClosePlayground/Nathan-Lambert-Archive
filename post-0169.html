More fresh food for locals doing it tough!

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

More fresh food for locals doing it tough! Thanks to Minister Luba Grigorovitch for joining us at DIVRS to announce $77,000 in funding from the Carroll Labor Government to expand their fresh food relief work. Through their partnership with Preston Market, DIVRS will use this funding for weekend donation markets, pop-up stalls and food rescue, helping more local families access fresh food. — with volunteers Dan, Vanessa and Kent; Ariyana and Christina from DIVRS; and Minister Luba Grigorovitch MP
