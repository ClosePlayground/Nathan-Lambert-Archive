Letterboxing the 61 Johnson St update because nobody watched my three-minute reel about it 😊

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Letterboxing the 61 Johnson St update because nobody watched my three-minute reel about it 😊 . TENANT COHORT The site will be for predominantly over-55s and is intended for tenants with low support needs. The community housing provider, National Affordable Housing Victoria (NAHV), will interview prospective tenants to determine suitability for a lower-support environment and whether they own a vehicle. PARKING The project will deliver 57 resident car spaces. This is more than the 48 spaces required under the planning scheme. While Darebin Council advocated for fewer spaces, we opposed this in response to concerns raised by surrounding residents. Homes Victoria has advised that the intended over-55s cohort tend to have less vehicles per household (approximately 0.4). Vehicle ownership will also be considered during tenant interviews. RETAIL AND FRESH FOOD Ground-floor retail is included, with the developer to determine its use based on market demand closer to completion of construction. We have asked for a fresh food grocer to be considered for the retail space at 61 Johnson, or potentially on the vacant land north of Keon Parade, adjacent to the rail corridor. HOUSING MIX AND MODEL While we recognise that some people would prefer a mix of larger apartments or a mix of social and private housing, the HAFF funding committed to this project is specifically for social housing. The proposed one- and two-bedroom apartments reflect demand on the Victorian Housing Register, particularly among the over-55 cohort. SAFETY AND MANAGEMENT Apartments have been repositioned to overlook the station plaza and pedestrian walkway, with improved lighting and sightlines to support passive surveillance. The management office has been relocated to the ground floor. NAHV will provide business-hours staffing on site, after-hours escalation and a direct contact community channel. We have also requested a full copy of Hallmarc’s consultation report and will provide further information when it becomes available.
