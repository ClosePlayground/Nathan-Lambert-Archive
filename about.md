<p class="intro">The Nathan Lambert Archive is an unofficial, static record of public Facebook posts and videos from Nathan Lambert, the Victorian Labor Member for Preston.</p>

## What this archive contains

The archive holds {{ site.data.archive.posts | size }} post records and {{ site.data.archive.videos | size }} video records. Each record has a permanent page with its date, text or description, and any links found in the export.

Dates are shown in Melbourne time. Records are ordered newest first on the archive pages, and grouped by year in the full index.

## Who is Nathan Lambert?

Nathan Lambert is the Victorian Labor Member for Preston in the Legislative Assembly. He was elected at the 2022 state election and represents the electorate that includes Preston and Reservoir.

He lives in Preston with his wife and three daughters. Before entering Parliament, he worked in the Victorian public service on forestry, energy and climate change policy. He grew up in regional Victoria and studied at the University of Melbourne.

His public posts cover local services, transport, housing, health, schools, parks and community events across the Preston area. This archive records that public Facebook activity and is not affiliated with Nathan Lambert, his office, the Victorian Government or the Labor Party.
