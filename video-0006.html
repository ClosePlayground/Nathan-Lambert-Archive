Video description

Over the last two weeks, I’ve received hundreds of emails and messages about the events in Israel, Gaza and the West Bank. We have all been horrified by the suffering and loss. Many government MPs spoke about the issue in parliament this week. Our contributions reflected our different perspectives, but we were united in our belief that the priority for the Victorian Government must be our own multicultural communities. Thousands of Palestinian and Jewish Victorians have friends or family caught up in the conflict. Many are fearful and anxious. Social media content can compound their anxiety. One-sided posts that fail to acknowledge Palestinian suffering circulate in our Muslim communities, and one-sided posts that fail to acknowledge Israeli suffering circulate in our Jewish communities. The priority for the Victorian Labor Government, as the Premier has made clear, is to provide love and support to all Victorians who have friends or family in Israel, Gaza or the West Bank, regardless of their religion or race. The last two weeks have been incredibly tragic for some, and traumatic for most. People who have spent their lives hoping for peace have seen it ripped away again. We have spoken with affected local residents. Our thoughts are with them. We will provide any support we can during this time of worry, suffering and grief.
