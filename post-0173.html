A re-elected Carroll Labor Government will cap train, tram and bus fares at $30 per week for two yea…

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

A re-elected Carroll Labor Government will cap train, tram and bus fares at $30 per week for two years — and cap concession fares at $15 per week. Other existing concessions, including free public transport for children and free weekend travel for seniors, carers and disability support pensioners, will continue.
