Our Victorian Labor Government has completed the rollout of 20 Women’s Health Clinics, including at…

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Our Victorian Labor Government has completed the rollout of 20 Women’s Health Clinics, including at the Austin, the Northern and the Royal Women’s Hospital. These clinics provide free specialist care for a range of women’s health issues, including endometriosis, pelvic pain, heavy and painful periods, menopause, prolapse, incontinence, contraception and abortion care. More than 17,500 women have already accessed these services across Victoria.
