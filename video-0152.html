The Reservoir Police Station counter service will close permanently from 6:00 pm this evening.

Source media filename: 1099703436569237.mp4

Video description

The Reservoir Police Station counter service will close permanently from 6:00 pm this evening. Those who attended the Darebin Neighbourhood Policing Forum earlier this month would have heard Victoria Police explain the rationale for the change. The aim is to ensure officers spend as much time as possible patrolling and responding to serious incidents. For emergencies or urgent police attendance, call 000. For non-urgent police assistance, call 131 444. Police reports can be made online through the Victoria Police website, at Preston Police Station between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm every day, or at Fawkner Police Station 24/7.
