One of our government's key reforms is making kindergarten free for four-year-olds and
three-year-olds, which has meant that a lot of families who previously might not have
sent their children to kindergarten at all are now sending them for two years. And
we know that has very significant benefits for those children and for their families,
but we also know it means we have to invest in our early childhood centres, not only
to provide the extra places, but also to provide the wraparound services that ensure
every child has the support they need. And so that's why we're absolutely thrilled
to join Minister Lizzie Blanthorne up at Keon Park Kindergarten on Arundel Avenue
with Poppy and the great team there to announce a very significant grant, $740,000 to upgrade
their facilities. We're also very pleased to be down here at JS Grey Kindergarten
announcing a significant upgrade here as well. And we look forward to working further
with the Minister's Office and with Darebin Council to upgrade our early childhood centres
right across Preston and Reservoir to ensure that every child has the best possible
start in life.
