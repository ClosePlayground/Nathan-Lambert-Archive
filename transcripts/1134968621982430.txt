Hi everyone, this is an update on the Bullance VFL Club. As many of you will remember, the AFL
terminated the Bullance licence in October last year and the club entered voluntary liquidation
in December. This week we're meeting with the AFL to ask them two key questions about that.
First, why was the licence terminated so abruptly, particularly given that in previous
seasons, notably in 2023, there had been the opportunity for a broader discussion and
for governments at all levels to provide some support. And then secondly, and probably more
importantly looking forward, how is the AFL going to guarantee the survival of standalone clubs,
either VFL or VFLW, that don't have access to pokies revenue and aren't affiliated with an AFL
club and cross-subsidised by them. Now, there are of course some important differences between
the VFL and the VFLW in terms of how they operate, but that key question about standalone
clubs is the same. We have been lucky locally that Kat Theophanous MP, our fantastic Labour
Northcote MP has secured some funding recently for the Falcons. Jed Carney has secured $700,000 for
change room upgrades and Mayor Emily Dimitriadis and Councillor Christina Laris, who was Mayor
last year, have secured some funding for lighting. So it's important to be clear that
overall the Falcons are in no way in the same position as the Bullance, but nonetheless,
it is still the AFL who make these key decisions about the future of VFLW clubs,
just as they do VFL clubs. And so we will take the opportunity to ask about both.
If you have any questions or issues related to that, that you think we should ask the AFL,
let us know. And then there is the second separate but related issue about the future
of Preston City Oval and its winter tenants. So over summer, the Preston Cricket Club have
been playing here. They'll continue to do so. They're going very well, but there have been
two suggestions made about winter. Firstly, there are of course two other Preston Bullance
clubs locally, the Juniors at Zwarr and the Amateurs at Ruthven. One option is for those
clubs to become winter tenants or at least play more games here and in that way preserve the
143 year legacy of the Bullance and potentially build the base for a VFL return. Alternatively,
there is the view that this is an elite level ground. It should host elite level sides. And
realistically, that's either the Northern Knights or Darabin Falcons VFLW or both.
Ultimately, that question is a question for Darabin Council. It's their asset.
It may be that if they're a bit more hands on in the way they manage it compared to
the past, they might be able to support both of those ideas to a degree. But if you have any
views on that, please contact your local councillor or send them to us and we can
send it to them. If you have any points for the AFL specifically, then let us know and we
can raise those with them this week.
