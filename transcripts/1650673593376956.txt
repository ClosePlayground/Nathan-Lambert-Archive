Hi everyone. We have an update on the 61 Johnson Street project, but before we get
to it, I just want to clear up one issue. For some reason, whenever we post about this
particular project, people comment to the effect that we're only building this housing
because we are in the pockets of the private property developers who are involved. And
I just want to say, obviously, that is not true. With respect to the local election
campaigns that we run here in Preston and Reservoir, we have never taken any money
from any property developer involved in any project. We are entirely funded by
our friends, by our families and by local Labor Party members. We're very grateful
for their support, but we're even trying to keep our costs down in this campaign so
that we can hopefully send some funds to our Labor colleagues who are battling it
out directly with the Conservatives and particularly those who are trying to hold
their seats against One Nation. And then on a personal note, I've certainly never
ever taken any form of gifts or corporate hospitality from a property developer. I
don't own shares in any of their companies. I don't own shares directly in any companies
at all, except through my superannuation. I don't own any investment properties, so
I don't have that potential alignment of interest. I've in fact only ever owned
one property full stop, and that is our current family home in Preston. And as
it turns out, I should say on this particular project, the developers who
involved Hallmark have unfortunately refused to respond to our calls and
emails about the project. So not only can we say, you know, we don't
generally hang out with these people, but in this case, we have literally not
spoken a single word to the developers. And as I say, I think that's actually
unfortunate because we did have some things to raise with them. But integrity
in politics is important. If you do at any stage have any concerns about any
Victorian politician, you can and should raise them with IBAC, the
Independent Board-Based Anti-Corruption Commission. We actually increased their
powers earlier this year to investigate exactly these sort of
issues. You can also raise any concerns with Victoria Police, with
the Victorian Ombudsman or with the new Parliamentary Workplace Standards
and Integrity Commission, which is another fully independent, well
resourced body that our government established in 2024. It is important
always to be vigilant about integrity in politics. And so should you need
them, we will put contact details for those organisations in the comments.
