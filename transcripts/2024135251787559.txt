Hi. As many people know, we've had a significant challenge with enrollment pressures here
at Preston High School. Obviously it's a relatively new government high school built
here on the old Preston Girls site back in 2019, and it was a very large investment
from the Victorian Labor Government, almost $50 million in total, in the new facilities
and the refurbished buildings. So it's generally a very good thing that those facilities
and buildings are being put to good use and are certainly already filled to capacity
with staff and students. But at the rate the school has been growing, there is
a very real risk that in the near future parents and families who are within the
zone would nonetheless start to miss out on places for their children. And so we're very
grateful to the Minister for Education, Ben Carroll, for not only securing the funds
to acquire the 233 Gower Street block that's just up there, but now securing a further
very significant investment to begin work on a second campus for Preston High. Now
we're still waiting on confirmation about the exact location, but before we get that,
I would just like to acknowledge the long-standing advocacy of many people on this issue,
including former school council presidents Arne May and Danielle Parker, current council
members Jane Melville and Chris Keating, Sean Butler, Jess James, interim principal
Emma Ford, and the Preston High students who raised this issue with us at our student
round tables. We are lucky to have the Minister for Education in our part of the
world later today for another event up in Reservoir, so we will take the opportunity
to thank him in person for this very significant Alan Labor Government investment
in our local public education.
