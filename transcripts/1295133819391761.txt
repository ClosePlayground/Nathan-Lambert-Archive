Just a quick reminder about the 20% rego rebate. As most people know, the state government has
responded to the cost of living pressures that have arisen due to the conflict in the Middle
East through two major measures. The first is making public transport free, as it will be for
the rest of this month, and then half price for the remainder of the year. And the second
is providing a one-off 20% vehicle registration rebate. Now this rebate will apply to light
vehicles registration payments that have been made in this financial year. So that is between
the 1st of July 2025 to the 30th of June 2026. So for most people, it will be a rebate on a
registration that you've already paid. If you've already paid, just to be clear, you've not
missed out, this is for you. Now it is available, however, only to individuals,
not to companies, and it is capped at two rebates per person. Applications will open
on Monday 1st of June through the Service Victoria app and website and close on Friday
31st of July. We'll put out a reminder once those applications open and some more details.
