Hi, some great news for Reservoir High. We've just confirmed with Principal Katie Watmo
and the school team that a re-elected Carroll Labor Government will complete Stage 3 of
the school's master plan. Now, as people will know, we've already done Stage 1, which
was the new design technology centre. We've also done Stage 2, of course, the science
and technology buildings. And then just this week, we finished rebuilding the Heidelberg
Teaching Unit, which is here at Reservoir High. I'm not sure why. It probably
should be called the Northern Teaching Unit, but students will be moving back in there
this week into that new building. And then finally, if re-elected, we will demolish
the old buildings A and D, which are two of the oldest school buildings anywhere in
Reservoir. And we will replace them with a brand new middle school that will mean
that the whole core of the school really is brand new buildings. It'll be a fantastic
outcome. But I have to be clear, this is an election commitment of a re-elected
Carroll Labor Government. It is under direct risk if Victoria instead elects
potentially a liberal one nation government. So if you have friends or family or
workmates who live in electorates that are a close contest between the Labor
Party and the Liberal Party, or potentially even a close contest with

education. It is Labor that will deliver full Gonski funding to our
public schools. It is Labor that has reached an agreement with the union to
give our public school staff better pay. And it's only Labor that invests
in our public schools as we've invested here at Reservoir High and
as we will continue to do so if we build this fantastic new final stage
three of the Reservoir High Master Plan.
