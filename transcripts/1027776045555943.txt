Deputy Speaker, Reservoir High School put on a wonderful performance of Matilda Junior
yesterday for students from Reservoir Views Primary, Preston North East Primary and Bandura
and Kingsbury Primary Schools. The whole cast and crew were fantastic, but I want to recognise
some standout performances from Aidan Turka and Bixie Wolford as the evil parents,
Alexia Kaloupis as Miss Trunchbull, Summer Dunn as Rodolfo, Hannah DeAngelis as Miss
Honey who sang exceptionally well, I thought, and Ada Yilmaz who starred as Matilda.
Thanks also to directors Nina Rossini and producers Rowan Bailey and Darryl Forbes.
Tickets are still available for Thursday and Friday night if members want to get along.
