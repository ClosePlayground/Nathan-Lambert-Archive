The Victorian Government is currently running a survey on age limitations for social media
and if you're at all interested I'd encourage you to fill it out.
It's important to be clear this is not about introducing fines or penalties for young people
for using their phones, there won't be any of that. Rather it is about introducing penalties
for the companies that make these applications and who allow young people to use them in ways
that we now know are harmful and the hope is that in the long run that will encourage them
to develop more age-appropriate versions of their technology. It is striking at the moment
in Australia that a 13-year-old can walk into an Apple Store for instance and purchase an iPhone
that can obviously access and do all of the things that an adult iPhone can do. That's not the case
in other countries and you can certainly imagine a world in which that 13-year-old
could still have a phone but there would be restrictions on the apps that could be downloaded
and there are restrictions on those apps themselves to ensure that the experience
was safer and more positive for that 13-year-old and the same goes for children
as they get older and older. So if you have any thoughts on what that might look like,
what the problems are, the possible solutions, how we get the balance right between the
positive and negative bits of social media, please fill out the survey. The deadline is
Friday, that's this Friday. The link will be in the comments or else you can just Google
Victorian Government Social Media Survey.
