Hi everyone, just a reminder that if you live east of High Street, so on this side, Sunday
is the deadline for Darebin Council's Your Street, Your State consultation on some important
road safety proposals, including new speed bumps on Hickford Street near Reservoir Views
Primary, a new raised crossing on Gower Street near Preston High, two new raised
crossings on Tyler Street near Preston North East Primary and Holy Name Primary, and
finally a new signalised pedestrian crossing here on Chenner Road. This here is the
current proposed location for that crossing just north of Crookston Road. I imagine
the big questions will be first, do you align the crossing with the intersection,
which does improve visibility for drivers, or do you move it even further south, making
it less about Rithven Station and more about Reservoir Primary School, noting that there
was a big petition at the school about it, does it go all the way down to Carroll Street
to fix that intersection there? If you have views on any of that or on the proposed
new 30 and 40 kilometre per hour zones that are part of the consultation,
you can provide them to Council before the Sunday deadline.
