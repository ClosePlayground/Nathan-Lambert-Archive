Hi everyone, we're in a Parliament this weekend. We have some more information about the speed limit changes that we mentioned.
Plenty Road is now formally under review to come down to 50kmph between Dundas and Albert Streets,
but there'll be no changes beyond Albert where it becomes the divided road.
Keon Parade is under review to come down to 60kmph. There will be no changes at the moment to
Marnies Road. It will stay at 70 and 80kmph and as far as we're aware there'll be no other
changes to state government roads in our area. However, Darebin Council is responsible for our
local roads and they've separately proposed that some of them come down to 40kmph or 30kmph.
As always, if you have any questions about these changes or any questions about road safety issues generally, just let us know.
