For a lot of people in Keon Park and Merrilands and the northern parts of Reservoir, the closest
emergency department is the Northern Hospital Emergency Department. And they're a fantastic
team there, but as any of us who have used the ED here know, it can get very busy at
times. And that's why, as a Victorian Government, we are building a much larger, brand new
emergency department. Stage one of that project is underway already. But it's also
why, as a Victorian Government, we've introduced the nation-leading Victorian Virtual
Emergency Department. Now, it happens to also be based here at the Northern Hospital
as well. In fact, I just learned today that it was invented here at the Northern Hospital.
They are a great team. We've just been talking to them. They provide an inclusive,
multilingual, culturally safe form of health care for people with mobility challenges,
for people with young children, for anyone for whom a trip to the physical ED might
be difficult or inconvenient. If you haven't already, you can just go to vved.org.au
and you can get really high quality, urgent health care across your screen and
device. And of course, by doing so, you are also helping to take the pressure off
and reduce the waiting times at our physical emergency departments.
