Hi everyone, we're here in Keon Park where we've got an update on the 61 Johnson Street
project that we're providing to people. I'll just run through it quickly.
With respect to the tenancies in the cohort, the site will be predominantly for over 55s
and is intended for tenants with low support needs. The community housing provider,
National Affordable Housing Victoria, will interview prospective tenants to determine
suitability for a lower support environment and also whether they own a vehicle.
And that brings us to parking. Unfortunately, we've not had a win there. In fact,
there will be 57 resident car park places, which is less than the 60 we had before.
It is still more than the 48 required under the planning scheme and more than the amount
that Darebin Council advocated for. But we have been advised by Homes Victoria that the
intended over 55s cohort tend to have less vehicles per household, approximately 0.4 per
household, and that vehicle ownership will be considered during tenancy interviews.
And then people have raised the issue of fresh food and the availability of fresh
groceries in the Johnson Street area. We have not had a win with that. There will be retail on
the ground floor, but we're still working on trying to confirm what sort of retail that will
be. With respect to the housing mix, the proposed one and two bedroom apartments remain.
We understand or are told that they reflect demand on the Victorian housing register,
particularly amongst this over 55s cohort. And then with respect to community safety
and management of the block, we've been told that the apartments have been repositioned
somewhat so that they better overlook the station plaza and the pedestrian
walkway that's behind me here. It will have improved lighting and sight lines.
The management office has been relocated to the ground floor and the community housing
provider will have people there Monday to Friday, nine to five, in normal business
hours if people have things that they want to raise. We have requested a copy of the
consultation report, though unfortunately we still haven't been able to obtain that.
But if you have any questions about this update, please just let us know.
