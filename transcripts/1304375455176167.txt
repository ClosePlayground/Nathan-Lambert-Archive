Deputy Speaker, we've been out door knocking homes near the Vizzi recycling plant on Radford
Road and I'd just like to put on the record a few further points about the odour issue
affecting some residents up there. The first is that Vizzi have made significant changes
to that plant in response to residents' complaints and action by our government's
Environment Protection Authority. You can see the extended stacks on top of the
plant. You can go inside and see the changes to ventilation systems, the bacteria
control, the recycled water and so forth. And most residents do agree that things
have improved. But a second very important point is just because things have improved
does not mean Vizzi is absolved from its continuing obligations under our Environment
Protection Act. There is still a smell there. I've smelled it myself. Now, admittedly,
different residents have different levels of sensitivity to that smell. Some people
don't think it's a problem but some people still think it is. And everyone has a
right to enjoy their home free from offensive odours. So I thank the Minister
and the great team at the EPA for their continued attention to this issue. I
understand that the EPA scientists were down there very recently having some
further conversations. Vizzi are a very large, very wealthy organisation
and we certainly look forward to some further action taken by them to
ensure that we have fresh and unpolluted air across that part of
Reservoir.
