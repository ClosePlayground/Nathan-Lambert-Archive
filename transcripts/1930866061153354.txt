We've had a few questions about the speed limit change on St George's Road so I thought
I would explain the new speed zone guidelines that came into effect last year. For divided
arterial roads like St George's Road, the recommended speed is now 60 kmph if there
is either kerbside parking, lots of driveways that back directly onto the road or a bike
path. So that change could potentially also be rolled out at Keon Parade and Marnies
Road where there are also a lot of driveways backing onto those roads or to Plenty Road
where it goes through Kingsbury. For undivided arterial roads where there's nothing between
you and the cars that are coming towards you on the other side, the recommended speed
is now 50 kmph where there is either significant kerbside parking or a tram line. Now that
will probably be applied to Plenty Road where it goes through Preston and Reservoir
because it has both of those things and maybe also Grange Road for people who head
down that way. The new guidelines also now allow 30 kmph zones in busy pedestrian
areas like Broadway, High Street Preston and Murray Road outside the Woolworths and they
also encourage more networks of 40 kmph local streets like you can see in parts
of Alfington and Westgarth. So Darribin Council is proposing to roll out both those
new 30 kmph and 40 kmph networks in their Your Street, Your Say consultation
so if you have any views on that make sure you get them in before Sunday when
that consultation closes.
