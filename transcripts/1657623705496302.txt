My question is for the Minister for Planning. My question is, if a development plan for
Preston Market were submitted for approval, at what point would that development plan
be made public? As you may recall, it was our government that put a heritage overlay
in place over Preston Market back in 2023. But at the same time that we did that,
we also put in place what is called a development plan overlay. That overlay means
that you cannot just go and get a planning permit to put a new building on that site
as you normally would. Instead, you first have to put together what is effectively a
site-wide blueprint for how the market's operations will be protected, how parking
will be provided, how pedestrian access will be provided and so forth. Now those blueprints
are not strictly binding on the planning permits that follow, but they are strongly
suggestive and I think there would be a great deal of community interest in such
a development plan if it were submitted. Any information that the Minister could
provide to us about how that process would work would be greatly appreciated by the
Preston community.
