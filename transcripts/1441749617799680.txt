As parents and staff know, Merrilands Early Learning Centre have let people know that they
will not be offering daycare next year. They will be switching to being a purely sessional kinder.
Uniting say this is because they've had difficulty filling places, but it is surprising to a lot of
people that a well-regarded, very long-standing non-profit centre would have difficulty filling
places, particularly in a part of the world where demand is actually growing. We'll speak
further with Uniting, we'll speak to Darebin Council and we'll try and find out why this
decision has been made and what support can be offered to affected families.
