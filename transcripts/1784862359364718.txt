Hi we're here at TW Andrews Reserve where we had a media conference today with the Minister
for Police to discuss two great announcements. The first is that a re-elected Carol Labor
government will deploy 1,500 more police officers across the state including in places
like Preston and Reservoir. And the second is that we will also introduce a new grant
program for local CCTV and security lighting. And of course this is an area where there
is a significant difference of opinion between the different political parties. The Victorian
Socialists for instance who are quite active in our area effectively want to abolish
the police. The Greens don't go that far but they want less police. And then as Labor
Party representatives we have the opposite view. We welcome today's announcement and
we very much hope to continue working closely with Reservoir Police, Preston Police and
of course with Darebin Council's law enforcement and compliance teams on a number of local
issues to ensure that our community can be as safe and secure and welcoming as possible.
