Just letting everyone know that the Reservoir Police Station counter service will close today permanently
and that the Preston Police Station counter service will now operate from 9am to 9pm seven days a week.
Now it's important to be clear that this does not at all mean that the Reservoir Police Station is closing.
In fact, there will still be exactly the same number of police officers here 24-7 as there have always been
and in fact it means that there will be more police available for the most important things they do
which is patrolling in the Reservoir community and responding to critical incidents when people need them.
They can't do that if they're stuck behind a desk.
Now, my brother used to work at the old Reservoir Police Station a long time ago
and I know that back then they would only have two officers patrolling all of Reservoir overnight
and then he or someone else would often be stuck behind the old front desk for the entire night
realistically not doing a great deal with their time.
Now we have a much bigger, better police station.
We have a dedicated family violence unit here and without going to the operational details
we certainly have more police doing more patrols more often and these changes will just further help that.
Of course, if you do need police for any serious reason just call 000.
