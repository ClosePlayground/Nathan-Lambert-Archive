Thank you, Speaker. My question is for the Attorney-General. I ask this question on
behalf of Addy, a student at Reservoir Views Primary School. Our question is, can a grade
three or grade four student be fined for littering? Addy asked this question during
a fantastic discussion with the grade threes and the grade fours about social and environmental
responsibility. I would like to commend grade three, four teachers, Connie Amoruso and
Che Baker for organising that discussion and recognise the very thoughtful and engaged way
that all the students approached both state and local issues. And also, while I'm here,
Speaker, I commend Haria Ali and Steve Stafford, the great leaders of Reservoir Views Primary
School. But I did take this particular question on notice because as the Attorney-General
knows, we have made some recent changes to the arrangements for fines for children
and we would be grateful if the Attorney could clarify whether the EPA or local councils
could issue a littering fine to a child of that age and how such a fine would be enforced
under the relevant acts. Thank you.
