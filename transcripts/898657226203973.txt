Hi, everyone. There's been some very sensationalist videos circulating recently on YouTube about
Reservoir Public Housing and Reservoir Public Housing tenants. We won't link to them, but
I think it's really important to say that the vast majority of people who live in public
housing are, of course, wonderful, great people who do a great job of looking after
their properties and, in fact, often leave those properties in better condition than
when they first moved in. When you do see some very extreme examples, and we're
here on Kirby Street where there is one of those rare extreme examples, what you
are looking at are some very acute mental health challenges, and our government is
committed to helping people who face those challenges. We have just this week opened
a new mental health local on High Street, and we're committed to implementing all
of the recommendations of the Royal Commission into mental health, and our office locally
works very regularly with Preston Housing and with other support services to make
sure that people get the help they need. If you're worried about a particular building
or location, if you're worried about the people who are living there, if you know
someone, then please feel free any time to get in touch with us, and we'll be
very happy to help out.
