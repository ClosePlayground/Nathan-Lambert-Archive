Hi everyone, some good news here at Preston City Oval, which is that the long-running
campaign to upgrade the lighting here has finally come together with the announcement
today of an additional $250,000 contribution from our Victorian Labor Government. And
as local residents will know, there are of course light towers here already, but
they're mainly old metal halide lights. They're very inefficient and they've been
too weak for clubs to train under. And in fact, the former Northern Bullants
VFL side, who of course were here, ended up spending tens of thousands of dollars
every season to hire grounds elsewhere so that they could train in the evenings.
And as history has shown, that was probably money they couldn't afford.
We do really wish in hindsight that the project could have come together a little
faster for them. But it is nonetheless great news for the Darebin Falcons VFL
side, who have been facing exactly the same issue, spending lots and lots of
money training at Latrobe and RMIT, Bundura and other grounds with decent
lighting. And it's also good news for the other two co-tenants here, the
Northern Knights and the Preston Cricket Club.
And while today's announcement is a state government announcement, a lot of
credit must go to Jed Kearney, our local Federal Labor MP.
It was Jed last year who secured seven hundred thousand dollars to upgrade
the change rooms here for female players and gender diverse players.
That is an upgrade that is very much needed.
And it was Jed's announcement that led to council bringing forward seven
hundred thousand dollars of their money for the lighting.
That led to the AFL providing another two hundred thousand dollar grant and now
to the state government providing our contribution.
And hopefully within that total budget, nearly two million dollars, the lights
and the change rooms can both be upgraded properly so that this fantastic
historic community facility can be a bit safer, more inclusive and better
utilised by the community.
And I know there are some discussions going on right at the moment on that
topic with Preston Bulance amateurs and juniors and other clubs about
increasing the local community use to make the most of this significant
investment. I do want to recognise quickly the contribution of Mayor
Emily Demetriades and local councillor Christina Laris in pulling
this project together. And then finally, on a different topic, we've also had
some good news from our meetings with the AFL and the Minister for Sport
regarding the systematic financial disadvantage faced by standalone VFL,
W and VFL clubs like the Bulance, like the Falcons.
