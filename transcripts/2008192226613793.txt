We've just attended a community safety forum held here at Preston Library on this wet and
windy day, organised by Jed Carney, our Federal MP, and we discussed a wide range of issues
which we'll summarise in the caption to this post. But if you have any thoughts
on any of them, please let us know because we are going to follow up everything that
people raised with the relevant ministers and organisations. And I would like to thank
everyone who attended, particularly Acting Inspector Michael Pope and Senior Constable
Peter Williams from Victoria Police, Simon and Craig from Northland, Kat Theophanous
MP, our Darebin Mayor Christina Laris, all of our great community organisations who
were there, and particularly those who came along and shared what were often quite
personal and difficult stories about crimes that had happened to them and their families.
Understandably, often those stories had a component that was critical of the way
the incident was handled or critical of regulations or policy, and it's not easy
to stand up and deliver those sort of criticisms face to face, but I thought people did
it in a very powerful and respectful way, and that feedback is very valuable to us
to feed into all of the reform that is happening in the community safety area,
especially at the state level, but also the federal and local level. So again,
if you have any thoughts on any of those changes to the laws or other improvements
that have been made to community safety, please just let us know.
