Acting Speaker, on a more positive note with respect to local water infrastructure and
environmental protection, I would like to thank Melbourne Water and Darebin Council for
releasing just yesterday their draft plan for a trail and some plantings along the Regent
Linear Reserve. This reserve sits above two water mains and it runs diagonally from the
Preston Reservoirs down to the southwest, over Spring Street, past the Rona Street playground,
past the JS Grey Reserve, past the Dunston Street Reserve, across Elizabeth Street and ultimately
through to Mary Creek. Acting Speaker, local residents have been long frustrated that this
particular reserve doesn't have a path on it, it doesn't have any plantings on it, aside
from a few river red gums I think near George Street, but it is mostly just unmoaned introduced
grasses and not only does it not have a path or any plantings or any significant plantings on it,
but it is fenced off and gated off at various places which actually makes it difficult
for local residents to get around their suburb and I know having spoken to people in the
Benambra Street area that there are some there for whom those gates mean that if they
have a pram or if they're in a wheelchair it is adding an extra 500 metres to their trip to
Newland Primary School or their trip to the Coburg Hill shops.
