Hi everyone, just letting you know the exciting news that we've just introduced the Statewide
Treaty Bill of 2025 to the Victorian Parliament. Those of you who attended the Gambu Gulen event
organised by Darebin Council the other week might have heard Uncle Andrew Gardiner talking about
this bill, but in essence it sets out the terms and conditions of the first treaty to be signed
between the State of Victoria and the First Peoples of Victoria. It's only been introduced
today, it won't be debated until a sitting week in October, so if you have any questions
about the bill as it stands, just let us know and we can provide that information.
If there's anything else you think we should raise in Parliament this week,
or raise with Ministers, just let us know and we'll follow up on that.
