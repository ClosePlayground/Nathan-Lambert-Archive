Hi, just letting you know that we have extended the consultation period on the proposed Preston
Market development plans by two weeks. So the new deadline is now 5pm on the 28th of
October. As people know, we had supported the initial shorter consultation period because
we were keen to get to a decision on the development plans ahead of the election.
We have been doing that on a number of local issues, not just on Preston Market
because we want to get things locked down and signed off so we have clear positions
and also to provide us with a bit of insurance against the possibility that there
is a change of government after November. Of course there remains the very real risk
in Victoria that we might see elected a liberal one nation minority government that would
do immense damage to so many things that we value here locally. But it was going
to be very tight timing regardless and we do understand that many people wanted
more time to make their submission. So you can now submit or resubmit your submission
on the Engage Victoria website up until the 28th of October. If you can't use
the website, please feel free to just come down to our office, we can help you,
we can submit on your behalf and we can organise translation services as we often do
for anyone who prefers to provide their feedback in a language other than English.
