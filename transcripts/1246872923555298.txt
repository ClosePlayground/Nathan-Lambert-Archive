Hi everyone. As you know, there were a number of serious safety incidents here at Northland
last year and one of the government's responses was to increase the number of police patrols
and protective services officers or PSOs. And we're grateful to the Premier and Minister
for Police for coming down here on Sunday to discuss that program with shoppers, businesses,
workers and the PSO team. And it was good to hear that they have seen some very positive
results in terms of customer confidence, worker confidence and with respect to reducing
incidents, particularly theft. I also think it's the credit of police and the PSOs involved
that they've been careful with their work to be mindful of the diversity of the community
here at Northland and have worked closely with support services where need be, including
alcohol and drug support, mental health support and youth support services. So
the feedback we've had has been very positive. The program will now be extended
to the end of the year. If you have any feedback or questions, please just let us know.
