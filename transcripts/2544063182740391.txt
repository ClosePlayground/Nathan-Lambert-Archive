Thank you Deputy Speaker. I'd like to begin by acknowledging the sad passing of Robert
Bob Cross, who played a very significant role in the Darebin community through his
leadership of Preston RSL and its success at the Darebin RSL. Bob joined what was
then Preston RSL in 1975 as a service member, following his own service, and then took
on leadership roles for almost half a century, guiding the club through a number of significant
challenges including the mergers that led to the Darebin RSL that we know today.
He made a huge personal contribution to the support of our local veterans, service
members, affiliate members and the broader community. Our thoughts are with his wife
Shirley, his family of course, and everyone across the Darebin community who will miss
his presence and leadership.
