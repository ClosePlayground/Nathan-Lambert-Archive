Hi everyone, National Cabinet is meeting at the moment to talk about petrol prices and
fuel supply, but ahead of that you might have seen that the Victorian Government has
announced that all public transport in Victoria will be free for the entire month of April,
and we're doing that because we know that if you make public transport free, a significant
number of people will choose to use it instead of their car, either for their
daily commute, perhaps they'll choose a tram over a taxi or Uber trip, and all
of that will reduce the number of car trips, reduce the demand for petrol and therefore
reduce the pressure on prices. Obviously as a state level Government we don't have
control over the international supply of fuel, but we have been working hard for many
years now to upgrade our public transport infrastructure so that it can be a more
popular option, reducing our dependence on cars, and this initiative builds directly
on all of that previous work. If you have any questions, feel free to get in touch
or check out the common Q&As in the caption below.
