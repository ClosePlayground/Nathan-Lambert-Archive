Hi everyone, we've just finished up in Parliament this week, and I just thought I'd let you
know that the major debates this week were about wage theft, worker screening, the administration
of drugs in aged care settings, and finally about the bail laws that I mentioned last
week. Although that latter debate was overshadowed by the tragic death of two police officers
in Port Punker, and there may be more to say about that incident, but for the time
being our thoughts are with their friends and family. On a local level I raised the
Eorick Street Apartments, enrolment pressures at Preston High, and also e-bikes on behalf
of local constituents, and we've put some information up in our stories about them.
And then finally the display that you can see behind me is put on each year by nursery
and garden industry Victoria, and I just want to thank Craig and David and Emily
and the team there for the great work they do to encourage MPs and staff and
decision makers to think about how we make Victoria a greener and more biodiverse place.
