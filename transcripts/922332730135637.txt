Hi everyone, just letting you know that a new urgent care clinic is open here at Your
Community Health on Separation Street and for a lot of people in Preston and the southern
parts of Reservoir, this will now be your closest free bulk build walk-in urgent care
clinic. If you are worried that you've got an urgent medical issue or worried about
a young child or someone else in your care, you can of course always use the state
government's new 24-7 virtual emergency department service, but these urgent care clinics do
have the advantage that they can perform a full in-person medical assessment, including
organising scans and tests from the bulk build facilities across the road and they
can then directly treat a lot of common issues, including minor infections, rashes, burns
and fractures. They're open here until 10pm every night, seven days a week, and while
these clinics were originally a state labour government initiative, we have to give
all credit here to our great federal labour MP, Jed Carney, who has delivered this
clinic here in Northcote for local community members.
