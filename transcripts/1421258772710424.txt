Thanks very much, Speaker. I take this opportunity to update the House on the fine work of Victoria
Police members across the summer, right across our state. In particular, we have seen at
Operation Pulse some 100 disruptive people being booted from our shopping centres across
Melbourne and the suburbs, some 109 arrests, 30 weapon sees and 215 offences detected
thanks to a $2.4 million program funded to support Victoria Police and the deployment
of additional PSOs and police across our shopping centre network at Northland, High Point and
at Fountaingate.
