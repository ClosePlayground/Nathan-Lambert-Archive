Local residents be aware of the good news that the Eric Street estate buildings, which
were in awful condition and where there were significant safety concerns, are now going
to be demolished. Now there was a long standing challenge here that we've mentioned previously
which was that Council had an old heritage overlay on the buildings that prevented
any demolition or redevelopment, but fortunately the State Government Minister for Planning
Sonia Kilkenny, we're very grateful that she has stepped in and used some new powers
that she has to give permission for this demolition to occur. Now that wasn't a
decision that was made lightly and there is a very detailed report about it that
we can send to anyone who's interested. It also doesn't mean that a final decision
has been made about the future of the site, but given that the specific powers that
the Minister used relate to social housing projects, we do think it is most likely
that we will see a new social housing project here on Eric Street at some point in the
future and that will continue the strong growth of social housing across Preston
and Reservoir that we've seen in recent years, including of course the site up
at Keon Park on Johnson Street that we posted about earlier today. As always, if
you have any questions about any of these projects, just let us know.
