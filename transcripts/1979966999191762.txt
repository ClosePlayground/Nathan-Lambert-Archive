Hi, some of you might remember that we announced last year that we had secured $200,000 to
plant 1,000 more trees across Preston Reservoir. And that was great news because historically
Preston and Reservoir have had relatively low levels of tree cover.
As it turns out, we've really learnt over the last 12 months that it is harder to
get trees in the ground than you might think. Particularly our public landowners
are often very nervous and very conservative about anything that might affect underground
infrastructure and their maintenance costs, and I do think there's some more policy
work to do around that. But the great news is that we have started and we have got trees
and plants in the ground right here at HB's Reservoir. A huge thanks to everyone who's
come down this week and helped with the planting, including Millie, Camilla, Alyssa,
Adrienne and Ben from Darebin, our Mayor Emily Demetriades, her children, Lorna,
the team from DECA and everyone else. And the other good news is that conscious that
there's an election coming up, we have made sure that we're not going to lose
any of this funding and we have an arrangement with Darebin Council where it will help
plant over a thousand trees and plants across Preston and Reservoir, and we'll put the
details of those additional planting locations in the comments of this post.
