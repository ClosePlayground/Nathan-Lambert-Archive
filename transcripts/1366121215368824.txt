Hi everyone, some great news this morning. Jed Carney, our Federal Labor MP has just announced
that she's secured over $3 million to support Darebin Council's high street upgrade. Now
we spoke about this upgrade about a year ago. It does have two controversial elements.
The first one is that it reduces the speed limit from 40 kilometres per hour to 30
kilometres per hour. We strongly support that because this is actually one of the
most incident-prone stretches of road anywhere in Preston or Reservoir. It has a large
number of incidents, particularly pedestrian incidents. Reducing the speed will mean
less incidents and less severe incidents when they happen. The second controversial
element is that it gets rid of the clearways and this will reduce the north-south vehicle
capacity in our part of the world. Again, we support that because we are slowly but
surely transforming this into a more pedestrian and bike-friendly precinct. However, it
does just remind us about the importance of making sure that when we do build new
housing in Darebin, as much as possible, we should build it close to public transport
hubs and train stations to make sure that new residents are using public transport
to get in and out of the city during peak hour and not adding to vehicle congestion.
