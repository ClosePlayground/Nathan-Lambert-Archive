Hi everyone. A few weeks ago we posted about the Woolworths car park upgrade on Murray Road
and a few people raised the important issue of pedestrian access from the Clinch Avenue side.
As it stands, if you're a pedestrian, if you've got a pram, if you've got a wheelchair,
your only way of accessing the supermarket is here via the vehicle roadway. We've spoken to
Woolworths about this, we've spoken to Darebin Council and we've spoken to the Minister for
Roads and Road Safety. The short story is because this upgrade was considered relatively
minor from a planning perspective, they did not need to do a complete redesign,
but we have asked them to ensure that next time they upgrade the car park, they provide safe,
dedicated, separated pedestrian access from Clinch Avenue. Woolworths have said that
they'll do that and we'll keep speaking to Minister and Council about making sure that
happens. Just wanted to provide people with that update.
