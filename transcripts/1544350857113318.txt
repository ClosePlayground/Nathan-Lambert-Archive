We were here at the Fantastic Sylvester Hive Community Garden in East Preston a couple
of weekends ago and one of the issues that people raised was the ongoing rumour that
the Pavilion School, the school behind us here, was closing down and I just want to
say that rumour is completely untrue. The school is not closing down and in fact there
have been no changes at all to the state government support for Pavilion or for other
flexible learning campuses. What has changed is two things. Firstly, there has been
an increase in the rent that the Pavilion School pays at its Epping campus, at its
other campus, and second there have been some financial challenges at Charles Latrobe
College which is the biggest school that Pavilion is part of and there have been some
staffing cuts and some public debate within the school community about how that has
affected Pavilion compared to other campuses. Now in response to those issues a reference
group has been formed or is being formed which will bring together people from different
parts of the school, different parts of the school community and seek in the first instance
just to establish a bit more of a common understanding of the facts here with regard
to the resourcing challenges and the staffing changes and then once it's done that it
will look at the bigger question of what the best governance model is for the Pavilion
School in the long run. But just to be really clear again the school is not closing
down and in fact we all recognise the really important role that it plays in our area
supporting students who have been disengaged from mainstream schooling. And finally a
quick thank you to Colin Brooks MP and Bronwyn Halfpenny MP, our colleagues who
have been working with us on this issue, particularly Bronwyn who's electorate covers
the Epping campus of Pavilion and who I know has been talking to a lot of staff, students
and families. If you have any questions at all about this issue just get in touch with
our office or Colin's or Bronwyn's.
