number of private long daycare centres that are opening up, including on Hughes Parade,

if that's what they would like. So we are asking Darebin Council again to partner
with us in expanding Jellybrand Crescent Kindergarten. Deputy Speaker, it's a great
community kindergarten. It has operated there since 1976. It has many long-standing staff,
including Centre Director Maria Soracchi, who I think has been there for 27 years,
Centre Coordinator Nina Cifonelli and Operations Manager Tracy Attard, who's also a long-term
local. Importantly, it's not only a great surface, but it also has a large site,

space. Deputy Speaker, we have previously raised West Preston and JS Grey Kindergarten
