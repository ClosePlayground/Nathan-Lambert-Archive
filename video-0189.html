Replacing roundabouts with raised intersections is a controversial road safety choice.

Video description

Replacing roundabouts with raised intersections is a controversial road safety choice. To Darebin Council’s credit, they are reviewing the Hickford–Borrie changes. We’ve asked the Minister for Roads and Road Safety for more information to inform the review and the consideration of other intersections, including: — Massey–Amery (changed to raised intersection in 2024) — McMahon–Black (changed to raised intersection in 2016) — McMahon–Boldrewood (potentially changing) — Massey–Broadhurst (potentially changing) — Glasgow–Godley (local residents are seeking a roundabout) — Arundel–Finton (new roundabout being installed). Thank you to Darebin Council and the Albanese Federal Government for their continued commitment to better road safety outcomes in our area.
