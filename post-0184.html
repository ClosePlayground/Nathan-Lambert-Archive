Welcome to Preston, LINK Community & Transport!

Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

Welcome to Preston, LINK Community & Transport! A special thanks to LINK volunteer and Reservoir local Mel Boak, who gave a great speech at the opening of their new headquarters about how LINK helps people stay independent and connected through community transport, social outings and support at home. If you’d like to join Mel and LINK’s other fantastic volunteers, or know someone who might benefit from their services, you can find all their details, including their new Water Road address, at lct.org.au. — with Mayor Emily Dimitriadis, Cr Cuc Lam OAM PSM, LINK CEO Fiona Fisher and Rhonda Withers
