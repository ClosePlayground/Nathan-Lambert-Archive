ERIC STREET UPDATE — the two public housing walk-ups at 15–17 Eric Street have been proposed for demolition. In the reel, I say that the Minister for Planning has given permission for demolition to proceed. To be completely accurate, a final decision hasn’t been made yet. Homes Victoria has completed the consultation and is preparing a planning application for demolition. If you have any questions or concerns, we can still follow up. Some other points of note from reading the reports… — The planning report says the vacant apartments are no longer suitable for housing due to their age, poor condition, fire damage, poor environmental performance and the difficulty of bringing them up to current building standards. — The heritage report says the statement of significance in the original heritage overlay was not accurate in describing the blocks as “an unusual development of pre-cast concrete walk-up flats”. Nonetheless, it recommends the preparation of an archival-quality photographic record in accordance with Heritage Victoria’s guidelines. — The preliminary arboricultural report assesses 24 trees within the site itself. It says the concept design may allow some trees in the central courtyard to be retained, including two mature melaleucas. Most trees on the site are exotics.
