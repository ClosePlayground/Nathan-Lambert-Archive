Nathan Lambert MP Shared from Instagram

PANDA MART UPDATE — while we’re scrambling to prepare Preston Market submissions, the Department of Transport and Planning has opened up consultation on another Preston landmark: the Panda Mart site. The proposal is to rezone the site to C1Z, paving the way for a mixed-use development with potentially 1,000 homes. We are pleased to see the environmental overlays remain in place, but are concerned about the proximity of the eastern buildings to the creek. It would also be good if the development facilitated a bike path connection to the creek from Water Road. Consultation is open until 2 November. See link in comments.
