PRESTON MARKET UPDATE — below are the further questions we have sent to the planning department. 15. There are lots of steps. How will the elevated levels be accessible for wheelchairs, mobility aids, prams etc? 16. Will local Fire Rescue Victoria teams need additional specialised equipment for the proposed tall buildings? 17. To what degree are the car parks ‘adaptive’, insofar as they could be turned into apartments, and under what circumstances would that happen? 18. How will rebuilding East Block with a structure above it meet heritage requirements? Could the design better preserve its heritage and identity? 19. For stage 2, the plans state: “Gas requirement for market expansion to be developed”. The ESD report says no new gas. Will there be any new gas connections across any stage? 20. When will the Green Travel Plan and public/active transport assessments be completed? 21. What will be the mix of apartments? 22. There is a risk of rat-running through the north-south streets, especially during peak hour. How will that be addressed? 23. Does Earle Street really need vehicle traffic? 24. What are the plans for the vacant government-owned land on the corner of Murray Rd and St Georges Rd? Can that site be used to provide parking in the first instance and more social or affordable housing in the long term? 25. Will every existing trader who wants to remain at Preston Market be able to return after redevelopment? How many traders will need to temporarily relocate during construction? What support will they receive? 26. What legal mechanism will guarantee that the privately owned public open spaces (including the elevated open spaces) remain publicly accessible in perpetuity? 27. How much direct sunlight will the existing market receive in winter compared with today? 28. Of the 811 car spaces associated with the market, how many will be available at each stage, and are these spaces shared with the supermarkets and other retail uses? 29. Will there be an in-house car sharing program for residents? (For the first 14 questions, see our earlier post. We’re hoping to receive answers to the first batch tomorrow.)
