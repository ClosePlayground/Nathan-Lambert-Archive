VISY UPDATE – if you are concerned about the odours from the Visy recycling plant, please sign the parliamentary petition via the link in the comments. BACKGROUND INFO Visy has been producing cardboard packaging at the Radford Road site since 1959. The recycling plant opened in 1983. There is some debate about when the odour issue started. Many people date it to roughly 2020, when Visy began recycling more water and using more household feedstock (which includes more food residue than industrial feedstock). Bacteria in the plant’s recycled water systems produced sulphides and volatile fatty acids, which can cause strong odours. Following complaints from residents, the Environment Protection Authority (EPA) issued a series of notices to Visy requiring the company to investigate and rectify the issue. The rectification measures included: — introducing a bacteria control program (from 2020) — increasing the use of fresh water for flushing (from 2021) — switching back to industrial feedstock (from 2022) — sealing roof vents and building gaps (2022–2025) — extending exhaust systems and installing new stacks and fans (2021–2026). Visy claims that the changes have resulted in a 91% reduction in measured odour units at the plant. However, we don’t believe the reduction experienced in the community has been anywhere near that amount. We have doorknocked all homes within 500 metres of the plant: once in 2022 and again this year. While different people have different senses of smell, the odours remain a serious problem for many households. In 2022, we estimated that more than 200 households felt the odours were having a major negative impact on their lives. In 2026, we estimate that more than 100 households continue to be affected that way. A local resident, Frieda De Doncker, has started a petition asking the EPA to impose further, urgent odour control requirements. We support that ask. We are sponsoring the petition to provide further evidence about the number of households affected. We will continue to raise the issue in parliament and with the minister.
