Aths track upgrade! A re-elected Carroll Labor Government will invest $500,000 to upgrade Edwardes Lake Athletics Track — home to Preston Reservoir Little Athletics Centre, Preston Athletic Club and (temporarily) Coburg Little Athletics Centre. We’ve secured an election commitment for new lighting and are looking at ways to incorporate new fencing as well. We’re also working with Reservoir police and Darebin Council on increased visibility and enforcement at the lake generally. — with Mayor Emily Dimitriadis, PRLAC president Lynton Martin, and newly appointed PRLAC centre captains and leaders Bastien, Ruby, Milla, Heidi and Walter
