HIGH STREET UPGRADE — our amazing federal MP, Ged Kearney, has secured $3 million towards a major upgrade of High Street between Bell Street and Murray Road. The project will include wider footpaths, more trees, safer pedestrian crossings, and more outdoor dining space. Thank you to Ged, Mayor Emily Dimitriadis and Cr Kristine Olaris OAM for their support of this project.
