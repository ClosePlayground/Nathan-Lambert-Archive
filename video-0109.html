LOCAL SPEED LIMIT CHANGES – some further information: — Plenty Rd is under review to come down to 50 kph between Dundas and Albert streets — there are no planned changes to Plenty Rd beyond Albert where it becomes a divided road — Keon Parade is under review to come down to 60 kph — there are no planned changes to any other state government roads in our area (Mahoneys, Bell, Murray, Spring, etc). Responsibility for roads in Darebin is divided between the state government (arterial roads) and Darebin Council (local roads). Darebin Council is separately proposing to reduce some local roads to 40 kph or 30 kph through its ‘Your Street Your Say’ process.
