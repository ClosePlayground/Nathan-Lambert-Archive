It’s rare to see a building in Melbourne left unfinished for 15 years. Building permits have mandatory timeframes attached to them, and councils can fine owners or refuse further permits if a site is left in a ‘dilapidated’ or ‘unsightly’ condition. To their credit, Darebin councillors revised their local laws earlier this year to explicitly extend those penalties to buildings that are left incomplete. Hopefully, the situation on the corner of Cheddar Road and Macartney Street will be resolved soon.
